# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating the candidate for full-time roles — scanning quickly, comparing many candidates, looking for signals of seniority and craft. Job to be done: decide whether to move the candidate to an interview.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that combines a resume-style narrative with embedded interactive case studies. Structured as a chronological flow (About, Experience, Projects, Skills, Education) where each professional experience entry can host the specific projects built during that role; each project is also reachable as its own standalone page for a deeper explanation. At least one embedded interactive tool/app serves as a live, hands-on demonstration of technical skill. Success = a recruiter can scan the whole story in ~30 seconds, then drill into any project that catches their interest, and comes away wanting to reach out.

## Brand Personality

Bold and distinctive — confident, opinionated, memorable. Reads like a senior practitioner with a point of view, not a template.

Structural reference: marcoditoro.com.br (scannable About/Experience/Projects/Skills/Education flow) and the site-feliz repo (Next.js + TypeScript + Tailwind + i18n stack) — borrow the section flow and stack, not the visual restraint. Visual identity should be its own, built around a lime accent (#92d00b) rather than minimalist neutrality.

## Anti-references

- Generic "developer portfolio template" look (stock hero + gradient blob, identical project cards, eyebrow labels on every section)
- Heavy or gimmicky animation — motion should be restrained and purposeful, not flashy

## Design Principles

- Show, don't tell — let real projects, embedded case studies, and the interactive tool demonstrate skill
- Scan first, dive second — the timeline reads in 30 seconds; every project is one click from a deeper case study
- Bold restraint — distinctive identity (lime accent, strong typography) without motion gimmicks
- One clear next action on every page (contact / view work)

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA — solid contrast, full keyboard navigation, `prefers-reduced-motion` support throughout.
